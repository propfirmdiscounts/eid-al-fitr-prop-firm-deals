# Eid Al-Fitr - PropFirmDiscount.com

Eid Al-Fitr prop firm deals and the discount codes of the firms that ran them.

## Dataset

14 deals from 10 prop firms, newest 2026-05-27.
Each row states the deal, its dates, the firm's discount code, and where to redeem it.
